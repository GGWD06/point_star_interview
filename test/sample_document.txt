ABOUT US
--------
Nova Dynamics Inc. was founded in 2019 by Dr. Elena Vasquez and Marcus
Chen in Austin, Texas. Our mission is to make smart-home technology
accessible and affordable for every household. As of 2025, we have
over 350 employees across three offices: Austin (HQ), Denver, and
Toronto.

PRODUCTS
--------

1. NovaSphere Hub — $129.99
   The central smart-home controller. Connects up to 75 devices via

RETURN & REFUND POLICY
-----------------------
- All products may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full
  refund, provided they are in original packaging and unused condition.
- Products returned between 31 and 60 days of delivery are eligible
  for a 75% refund or full store credit.
- After 60 days, no refunds are offered. Warranty claims must be filed
  instead.
- Refund processing takes 5–10 business days after we receive the
  returned item.
- Shipping costs for returns are covered by Nova Dynamics for defective
  items. For non-defective returns, the customer pays return shipping
  (estimated $8.95 flat rate within the US).
- Subscription services (e.g., NovaGuard cloud storage) can be
  cancelled anytime with a prorated refund for the remaining month.

WARRANTY
--------
All hardware products carry a 2-year limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects. The warranty does NOT cover damage from misuse,
water exposure, or unauthorized modifications. To file a warranty
claim, contact support@novadynamics.com with your order number and a
description of the issue.
