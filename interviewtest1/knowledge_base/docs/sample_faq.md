# Customer Support FAQ

**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Account & Login

**Q: I can't log in to my account. What should I do?**  
A: First, try resetting your password using the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. An email will be sent to your registered address within 2 minutes. If you don't receive it, check your spam/junk folder. If the issue persists after 15 minutes, contact support at support@company.com.

**Q: How do I change my email address?**  
A: Go to **Settings → Account → Contact Information**. Enter your new email and verify it by clicking the confirmation link sent to the new address. Note: you'll use the new email for login immediately after verification.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**  
A: Account deletion requests can be submitted via **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. Deletion is permanent and irreversible. All data will be removed within 30 days per our Privacy Policy. Active subscriptions must be cancelled before deletion can proceed.

## Billing & Payments

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**  
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and bank transfers (ACH for US customers). We do not accept cryptocurrency or prepaid gift cards.

**Q: Why was I charged twice?**  
A: Duplicate charges can occasionally occur due to payment processor errors. Email billing@company.com with your Order ID. We will investigate and refund any duplicate charge within 5 business days.

**Q: When does my subscription renew?**  
A: Your subscription renews on the same day each month/year as your original purchase. You'll receive a renewal reminder email 7 days in advance. You can view your next renewal date in **Settings → Billing**.

**Q: Can I get an invoice for my purchase?**  
A: Yes. Invoices are automatically generated and emailed after each payment. You can also download past invoices from **Settings → Billing → Invoice History**.

## Technical Support

**Q: The application is running slowly. What can I do?**  
A: Try these steps in order:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies.
2. Try a different browser (we recommend Chrome or Firefox, latest version).
3. Disable browser extensions — some extensions interfere with our app.
4. Check your internet connection speed.
5. If the issue persists, report it via **Help → Report a Bug** with your browser version and OS.

**Q: I'm getting an error code. What does it mean?**  
A: Common error codes:
- **Error 401**: Authentication failed — your session may have expired. Log out and log in again.
- **Error 403**: Access denied — you don't have permission for this resource. Contact your account administrator.
- **Error 500**: Internal server error — this is on our end. Check our status page at status.company.com for ongoing incidents.
- **Error 503**: Service temporarily unavailable — we may be performing maintenance. Try again in 10–15 minutes.

**Q: Which browsers are supported?**  
A: We support the latest two major versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Internet Explorer is not supported.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**  
A: Yes. Our mobile app is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). The mobile app supports all core features. Some advanced admin features are only available on the web version.

## Data & Privacy

**Q: Where is my data stored?**  
A: All customer data is stored in data centers located in the United States (primary) and the European Union (for EU customers), in compliance with GDPR. Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

**Q: Can I export my data?**  
A: Yes. Go to **Settings → Privacy → Export Data**. A download link will be emailed to you within 24 hours. The export includes all your account data in JSON format.

**Q: How do I report a security vulnerability?**  
A: Please email security@company.com with details. Do not post vulnerability information publicly. We operate a responsible disclosure program and will respond within 48 hours.

## Plans & Upgrades

**Q: What plans are available?**  
A: We offer three plans:
- **Starter** ($19/month): Up to 3 users, 5 GB storage, email support.
- **Professional** ($79/month): Up to 25 users, 50 GB storage, priority support, API access.
- **Enterprise** (custom pricing): Unlimited users, custom storage, dedicated support, SLA guarantees.

**Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?**  
A: Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately — you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Go to **Settings → Billing → Change Plan**.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**  
A: Yes. All new accounts receive a 14-day free trial of the Professional plan. No credit card is required to start. You'll be prompted to add a payment method before the trial ends if you wish to continue.
