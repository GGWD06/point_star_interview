# Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

---

## 1. Eligibility for Refunds

Customers are eligible for a full refund if they meet **all** of the following conditions:

- The refund request is submitted within **30 days** of the original purchase date.
- The product or service has not been fully used or consumed (applicable to subscription plans: less than 50% of the billing period has elapsed).
- The account is in good standing (not suspended for violation of Terms of Service).

Refunds are **not available** for:

- Purchases older than 30 days from the original transaction date.
- Add-on purchases (one-time feature upgrades, API credits, premium support packages).
- Custom enterprise contracts — refunds are governed by the individual contract terms.

---

## 2. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, customers must:

1. Contact support at **support@company.com** with the subject line: `Refund Request — [Order ID]`.
2. Include the **Order ID**, the **email address** used at purchase, and a brief reason for the refund.
3. Allow up to **5 business days** for the request to be reviewed.

Refund requests submitted via live chat or phone are **not accepted** — only email requests are processed.

---

## 3. Refund Processing Timeline

| Scenario | Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Credit/debit card | 5–10 business days after approval |
| PayPal | 3–5 business days after approval |
| Bank transfer | 7–14 business days after approval |
| Store credit (alternative) | Issued within 1 business day |

---

## 4. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be granted at the company's discretion for:

- Subscription plans where between 1–50% of the billing period has elapsed. The refund amount is prorated for the unused portion.
- Cases where the customer experienced a verified service outage lasting more than **4 consecutive hours** during their billing period. A prorated credit for the outage duration will be applied.

---

## 5. Non-Refundable Circumstances

The following are explicitly **not eligible** for refunds under any circumstances:

- Dissatisfaction with features that were accurately described in the product documentation at the time of purchase.
- Failure to cancel a subscription before the renewal date (customers are sent a renewal reminder 7 days in advance).
- Refund requests made after the 30-day window has passed.
- Accounts banned for Terms of Service violations.

---

## 6. Disputed Charges / Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is unauthorized, please contact us **before** initiating a chargeback with your bank. Initiating a chargeback without first contacting support may result in immediate account suspension.

To dispute a charge: email **billing@company.com** with your Order ID and a description of the issue.

---

## 7. Policy Changes

This refund policy is subject to change. Customers will be notified via email at least **14 days** before any material changes take effect. Purchases made before the effective date of a change remain governed by the policy in effect at the time of purchase.
